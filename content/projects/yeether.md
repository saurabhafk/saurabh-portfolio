## Overview

Yeether is an ongoing React Native product at Appinventiv Technologies focused on monetization, secure onboarding, and scalable UI.

## Highlights

- Google and Apple sign-in for a seamless onboarding flow
- React Native Keychain for tokens in iOS Keychain and Android Keystore
- Google Mobile Ads and in-app purchases for monetization
- Pixel-perfect, device-agnostic UI across phone sizes
