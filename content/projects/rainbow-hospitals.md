## Overview

Rainbow Hospitals is a patient-facing React Native experience spanning scheduling, telehealth, and personalized health features.

## Highlights

- Device-agnostic UI/UX for consistent experience across platforms
- In-person and virtual consultation scheduling
- Real-time video consultations with Agora
- Custom chat and calendar scheduling flows
- Period tracking feature for preventive, personalized care
